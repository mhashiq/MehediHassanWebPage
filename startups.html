How the company got here
OCTOBER 2020

Founded as The Virtual BD

Started as a full-service IT consultancy building enterprise web applications, WordPress solutions, eCommerce infrastructure and digital strategy for international clients. Delivered 100+ client projects end to end, holding a consistent standard for clean architecture, SEO performance and high availability, and grew cross-functional teams across UI/UX, full-stack engineering and content strategy.

JANUARY 2025

Relaunched as AuthBrain

Repositioned from a client-services consultancy into a product company. The consultancy's delivery experience was folded into a single platform pairing predictive AI with zero-trust authentication, aimed squarely at regulated healthcare and financial institutions rather than general web work.

Products

Adaptive Risk-Based Access Control

Evaluates every access request in real time against session behaviour, device signatures and network anomalies.

Sub-50ms decisions, with no added friction for legitimate sessions.

Biometric & Graph-Based Fraud Detection

Maps facial feature vectors onto transaction graphs to expose coordinated identity-theft networks.

Identifies hidden identity syndicates before fraudulent transactions settle.

Automated Medical Imaging Analysis

Performs 3D organ and tumour segmentation directly from radiological scans.

Turns multi-slice studies into actionable surgical boundaries in seconds.

Workflow Productivity & Snippet Tooling

Organises technical team workflows with encrypted credential and code-snippet histories.

Protects local technical state while speeding up developer context switches.
Who it serves

Healthcare

Clinical decision support, diagnostics, readmission-risk forecasting and population-health stratification.

Finance

Fraud prevention, synthetic-identity detection and regulatory auditing.

Enterprise

CTOs and CISOs consolidating authentication and predictive intelligence into one system.

Deployment & capabilities

Every deployment runs on-premise or inside the customer's private cloud, so regulated data never leaves their own boundary — without trading away speed, precision or compliance.
