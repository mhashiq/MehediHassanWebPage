About

I am a Graduate Research Assistant in Medical AI in the College of Health & Human Sciences at Texas Tech University (Ph.D. student, 2026–present). I work in the Causal Dynamics Lab and serve as Lab Manager in the SI Research Lab.

My research builds patient-level world models that combine ECG, chest X-ray, CT and clinical risk factors to forecast cardiovascular disease and simulate the effect of treatments. I also work on medical imaging for diabetes, obesity and fatty liver disease.

I hold M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in Computer Science and Engineering, have edited 8 books with Springer, CRC Press and River Publishers, and hold two granted Indian patents (Nos. 382600-001 and 382599-001). My Field-Weighted Citation Impact is 2.09 (SciVal), about twice the world average.

Research Themes

Want to see my current project on world models?

What is a world model? → See CardioPath working →

World models

Patient-level models that learn how cardiovascular health changes over time and simulate “what if” scenarios such as quitting smoking or starting statins.

Cardiovascular AI

Forecasting ECG findings and 10-year risk from multimodal data, with explanations checked against the evidence behind each prediction.

Medical imaging

Lightweight 3D segmentation of the liver, brain tumours and lesions from CT and MRI, built to run fast on modest hardware.
